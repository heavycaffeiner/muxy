# muxy 0.119.8

## Touch behavior

Restore the original Termux tap policy instead of the custom double-tap click gesture:

- With mouse reporting enabled, one touchscreen tap sends an immediate mouse press/release pair on finger release. It does not request the keyboard.
- Without mouse reporting, a confirmed single tap opens the keyboard on either primary or alternate screens.
- Double tap has no custom action. With mouse reporting, the two taps produce two ordinary clicks. Without mouse reporting, double tap does not open the keyboard.

Each contact records its emulator, input generation, mouse-reporting state, and active screen. If these change during the gesture, the old action is discarded. A TUI click that disables mouse reporting or exits the alternate screen cannot become a delayed keyboard request. A new tap on the resulting shell works normally.

Drag, hold, cancellation, multitouch, selection, physical mouse, and touchpad paths remain separate. Keyboard buttons and shortcuts remain available while mouse reporting is active. The terminal protocol does not expose TUI widget focus.
